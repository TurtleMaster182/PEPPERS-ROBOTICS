[ Us ]

A team that builds.

Peppers is a student-and-alumni STEM community from Liceul Teoretic de Informatică “Grigore Moisil” in Iași. Mechanical design, electronics, software, and outreach all happen in one workshop.

[ Mission ]

Bring the heat. Share it.

We give students room to design, build, break, and rebuild real machines. Then we share what we learn through education, mentoring, and community projects.

[ FTC ]

What is FIRST Tech Challenge?

An international robotics competition for students aged 12–18. Each season starts with a new game. Teams design, build, and program a robot to play it, then compete in alliances at regional, national, and world events.
