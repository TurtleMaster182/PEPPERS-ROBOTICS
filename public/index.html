04 / Inside the process

Big ideas.
Lots of small steps.

  1. 01

    Figure it out.

    Understand the challenge. Sketch, discuss, and work through the possibilities.

    Think & design
  2. 02

    Make it real.

    Put the first version together. Connect the mechanical, electrical, and software work.

    Build & program
  3. 03

    Put it to the test.

    Try it on the field. See what works, find what doesn’t, and make the next adjustment.

    Test & improve
  4. 04

    Bring the heat.

    Bring the whole team together for competition — then take the lessons back to the workshop.

    Compete & repeat

Peppers Robotics is a student-and-alumni STEM community and FIRST Tech Challenge team from Iași, Romania, based at Liceul Teoretic de Informatică “Grigore Moisil”.

Mechanical design, electronics, software, and outreach come together in our workshop. Beyond competition, we share robotics through education, mentoring, and community activities.
